# The Hunger Games

**The Hunger Games** is a dystopian science fiction movie series based on the novels written by Suzanne Collins. It follows the story of Katniss Everdeen, a young woman who becomes a symbol of rebellion against the oppressive government of Panem.

---

## About the Series

The story takes place in Panem, a nation divided into 12 districts ruled by the wealthy and powerful Capitol. Every year, the Capitol organizes an event called the Hunger Games, where young people from each district are forced to fight for survival in a televised competition.

Katniss Everdeen volunteers to participate in the games to protect her younger sister, Primrose. Throughout her journey, she faces challenges, discovers the truth about her society, and becomes the face of a revolution.

---

## Main Characters

- **Katniss Everdeen** – The main protagonist who volunteers for the Hunger Games to save her sister.
- **Peeta Mellark** – A fellow tribute from District 12 who develops a close relationship with Katniss.
- **Gale Hawthorne** – Katniss's best friend and hunting partner.
- **Haymitch Abernathy** – A former Hunger Games winner who mentors Katniss and Peeta.
- **Effie Trinket** – The Capitol representative assigned to District 12.
- **President Snow** – The ruthless leader of Panem.
- **Primrose Everdeen** – Katniss's younger sister and the reason she volunteers for the games.

## Why I Like This Series

I like The Hunger Games because of its exciting storyline, intense action, and meaningful characters. It shows the importance of courage, survival, sacrifice, and standing up for what is right. I also enjoy how the story combines adventure, romance, and political conflict.

---

## Important Themes

- **Survival** – Fighting to stay alive despite difficult circumstances.
- **Courage** – Standing up against fear and injustice.
- **Love and Sacrifice** – Protecting the people who matter most.
- **Freedom** – Fighting for a better future.
- **Rebellion** – Challenging an unfair and oppressive government.
